03 · Monetization

How an algorithm turns the profile into your price

Every pricing model asks the same question: at each price, how likely is this person to say yes? With no data it can only use the average customer, so everyone gets the list price. With a profile it estimates your curve, and the revenue-maximizing price moves toward the most you would tolerate. Drag the sliders.
